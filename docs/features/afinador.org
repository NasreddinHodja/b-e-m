#+TITLE: Afinador
#+OPTIONS: ^:{}

Microphone tuner: note, cents, stability, needle, sparkline. Not scored.

Files: =(app)/afinador/+page.svelte=; =features/afinador/= =autocorrelate.ts=, =tuner.svelte.ts= (=tuner=).

* Flow

+ /Ativar microfone/ :: mic without echo cancellation, noise suppression or auto gain; its own =AudioContext=; 60 Hz high-pass into an analyser (4096). Refused: /Microfone bloqueado/.
+ Every frame :: =autoCorrelate()=; readings outside the mode's range are dropped (chromatic 27.5–4200 Hz, guitar 80–1400, voice 85–1100), the rest smoothed. Shows the nearest note against /Ref. A/ (default 440, not clamped), cents ±50, stability over 20 readings, 120 readings of history.
+ ±5 cents :: in tune (green); sharp gold; flat red.
+ The mic stays on across pages until /Desligar microfone/ (legacy).

* autoCorrelate(buffer, rate)

Hz, or −1 for silence (RMS < 0.018). Trims weak edges, autocorrelates, takes the highest peak after the first descent, refines it parabolically.
