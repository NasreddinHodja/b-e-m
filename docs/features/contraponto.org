#+TITLE: Contraponto (Fux)
#+OPTIONS: ^:{}

Write up to three voices against a cantus firmus, in first or second species, as text or on a piano roll; the rules are checked as you type, the voices play back, and the score exports to LilyPond. Not scored.

+ Page :: =src/routes/(app)/contraponto/+page.svelte=; =Roll.svelte= (the piano roll), =colors.ts= (voice colours)
+ State :: =src/lib/features/contraponto/contraponto.svelte.ts=, =contraponto=
+ Rules :: =src/lib/features/contraponto/rules.ts=
+ Export :: =src/lib/features/contraponto/lilypond.ts=

* Voices

=contraponto.voices= is 2–4 voices of ={ cf, sp, on, txt }=: voice 0 is the cantus firmus (=cf=, always first species), the others have a species (1 or 2), a /som/ checkbox (=on=, audible in playback) and their notes as text. =active= is the voice the roll edits (the radio button, and the gold outline).

Notes are tokens separated by spaces or commas: letter, accidentals (=#= / =b=, any number), octave; =C4= is middle C (=parse()=). In second species =r= or =-= is a rest (=readVoice()=). Each note becomes ={ n, dia, midi }=: its spelling, its diatonic step (=octave·7 + letter=) and MIDI number. Intervals are measured in both, so a spelling matters (an augmented second isn't a minor third).

A first-species voice has one note per cantus note; a second-species voice two, except the last, so =2n − 1=.

Actions:

+ /Exemplo 1ª esp./, /Exemplo 2ª esp./ :: the cantus =D4 F4 E4 D4 G4 F4 A4 G4 F4 E4 D4= with a correct example voice. The page opens on the first.
+ Species select :: doubles every note but the last (1 → 2), or keeps every other one (2 → 1).
+ /+ voz/ :: up to 4; a new voice is the cantus an octave up, first species.
+ /×/ :: removes that voice.
+ /+ compasso/, /− compasso/ :: repeats every voice's last note (twice in second species) or removes the last measure; not below 2 cantus notes.

Every change to the notes regenerates the LilyPond text (=#changed()=, the legacy =run()=); the textarea can be edited by hand in between.

* The roll

=Roll.svelte= draws the voices as an SVG: columns are half-measures (=CW= 34px), rows semitones (=RH= 10px), with lines on the natural notes, labels for the pitches in use, dashed measure lines, the active voice's intervals with the cantus (diatonic numbers) along the bottom, and red (error) or amber (warning) rings on the notes the issues point at.

Pointer down in a column picks the voice: the active one, unless another voice has a note within a semitone of the pointer there. Dragging then sets that voice's note in that column (=slotAt()=, =place()=), spelled on the nearest natural with the accidental from /Ao inserir nota/ (=spellNear()=), and previews it with the cantus note under it. The pitch range is frozen while dragging so the roll doesn't rescale under the pointer.

* Rules

=analyze(cf, cp, sp, above)= checks one added voice against the cantus; =above= is whether its average pitch is at or above the cantus's. Everything is an error (=erro=) unless marked as a warning (=aviso=):

+ The cantus needs at least 2 notes; the voice needs exactly =n= (first species) or =2n − 1= (second) notes, or nothing else is checked.
+ Rests only at the start.
+ Voice crossing (warning).
+ No dissonance on a strong beat (every note in first species, the first of each pair in second).
+ Second species: a dissonance on the weak beat only as a passing note (step in, step out, same direction).
+ Unison only at the start and end (strong beats).
+ Start on a perfect consonance (unison, fifth, octave); end on a unison or octave (a fifth at the end is a warning).
+ Cadence (warnings): first species, the penultimate note a major sixth above, or a minor third below, the cantus; second species, the penultimate measure starts on a fifth and its second note is that sixth or third.
+ Parallel fifths and octaves, and direct motion into a perfect interval from a perfect or an imperfect one, strong beat to strong beat in adjacent measures. In second species, a weak-beat leap of a fourth or more between them excuses it.
+ Melody, note to note: augmented and diminished seconds, thirds, fourths, fifths and octaves; the tritone; sixths, except a minor sixth upward; sevenths; leaps beyond the octave; a chromatic step on one letter (warning); a repeated note in second species (warning).
+ Melody, three notes (warnings): the same note three times (first species), two leaps of a third or more in the same direction, a leap of a fourth or more followed by more motion the same way.
+ Range over a tenth (16 semitones) (warning).

=pairsCheck()= checks each pair of added voices, downbeat to downbeat: parallel fifths and octaves and direct motion between perfects (errors), direct motion from imperfect to perfect (warning).

Unparseable notes in any voice replace all of this with one error naming them. The summary line above the roll: /Digite ou desenhe o contraponto./ when voice 2 is empty, else the error and warning counts (red when there are errors), or /Nenhum problema encontrado./ (green).

* Playback

/▶ Tocar/ schedules every audible voice at once from =audio.now() + 0.1=: a half-measure is one beat at /Andamento/ (40–160), first-species notes and the final note last two. A gold playhead follows on the roll (=requestAnimationFrame=, =head=). /repetir/ loops; /■ Parar/ stops the playhead (notes already scheduled still sound, as in the legacy app).

* LilyPond

=generateLilyPond()=: LilyPond 2.26.0, =\time 2/2=, =\tempo 2 = <bpm>=, one variable per voice (=cantusFirmus=, =voz2= …) with bar lines after each note (first species) or each pair, a =StaffGroup= with instrument names, the bass clef for a voice more than 6 semitones below the cantus on average, =\layout= and =\midi=. /Gerar LilyPond/ regenerates it; /Copiar .ly/ copies the textarea; /Salvar .ly/ downloads it as =bem-contraponto.ly=.
