* TODO Monospace everywhere
Every text is DM Mono, including prose (Manifesto, feedback, hints, the journal), where it reads worse.

** Plan
Map every mono use, view by view, and sort them: keep mono where it carries meaning (note and interval symbols, counters, tempo, LilyPond and note-entry fields, maybe small uppercase labels); use a readable sans or serif for prose and controls. Present the map and a font pairing for the owner to pick before changing anything: the choice of fonts is theirs.

* TODO Icons
The "icons" are Unicode and emoji glyphs (♬ ↔ ⚖ ◈ ◇ ♪ ◎ 📓 ⏱ 📖 ○ 🚪 📱 🎧 ▶ ⏹ 🗑 …), rendered at text size, inconsistent across systems, too small.

** Plan
An icon library (Lucide, through =@lucide/svelte=, is the likely pick: tree-shaken SVG components, consistent stroke), sized to the text they sit with (16–20px in nav and buttons) and labelled for screen readers where they stand alone. Needs the owner's OK on the library and to install it.
